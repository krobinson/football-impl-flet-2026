## Summary

Remove four navigation tabs (History, Teams, Matches, Records) from the app's bottom navigation bar, leaving only **2026 Map**, **Venues**, and **Schedule**. The change is confined entirely to `src/app.py`: remove 5 imports, 4 content-block variables, the `store` variable, and simplify the `_build_app()` signature and `tab_bodies`/`nav.destinations` lists from 7 to 3 entries. No source files are deleted; no tests are added or modified.
